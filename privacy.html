SnapDex

Privacy policy

Version 22 October 2026

What SnapDex stores, where, who processes it, and how to get it deleted.

Who is responsible

RmDesign runs SnapDex and is responsible for your account data. Email hello@snapdex.app about anything in this policy. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

What we collect

  • Account: your email, name and how you sign in (email, Google or Apple).
  • Profile: the industry and networking goals you choose, and your My Card details.
  • Content: photos you scan, the details read from them, contacts, events, event goals, notes and debriefs.
  • Calendar: if you connect it, SnapDex reads upcoming event titles, times and locations on your phone to suggest events. Your calendar is not uploaded.
  • Voice search: your phone turns speech into text, and only the text of your question is sent to SnapDex.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with Google Firebase (Google Cloud) in the London region (europe-west2). Photos are kept in Firebase Storage under your account.

AI processing

SnapDex uses Claude, made by Anthropic, for its AI features. This only happens if you turn on card reading, and requests are sent from SnapDex's servers, never directly from your phone. Anthropic acts as our processor.

  • Card reading: the scan image, plus your industry and goals.
  • Debriefs: the details read from that event's scans and the event's goals.
  • Follow-up email drafts: the contact's name, the reason for following up and your name.
  • Search: your question and a short list of your contacts (name, company, title, industry, notes).

With card reading turned off, scans are stored in your account and nothing is sent to Anthropic. Debriefs, drafts and search only run when you tap them. AI output can be wrong, so everything Claude writes is marked in the app. You can turn card reading off at any time in Settings, Privacy and AI.

Why we use it

To provide the service you asked for (contract), and, for AI features, because you turned them on (consent). You can withdraw consent at any time in the app. Scans you take afterwards are saved but not sent for reading.

Sharing

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. It is shared only with the processors named here (Google Firebase and Anthropic), with teammates you choose (below), and if the law requires it.

Team events

If you create or join a team event, everyone in that team (up to five people) can see the scans filed under that event, including the photos, the details read from them and any notes on them, plus your first name. A team debrief uses everyone's scans from that event. Contacts still go only into the network of the person who scanned them. Leaving the team stops you seeing the event; the scans you took stay with the team until the event is deleted.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. If you delete your account you can choose immediate deletion, or deactivation with 30 days to change your mind, after which everything is deleted.

Deleting your account and data

In the app: open the You tab, then Account and data. Choose "Delete account and everything now", or "Deactivate, delete in 30 days" if you'd like time to change your mind.

Without the app: email hello@snapdex.app from the address you sign in with and ask us to delete your account. We'll confirm, then delete your account, scans, photos, contacts, events and debriefs within 30 days.

Deleting your account removes it from any team events you joined. Scans you added to someone else's team event are deleted with your account.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, object to processing, and withdraw consent. Most of this you can do in the app. For anything else, email us.

People on the cards you scan

If your details were scanned by a SnapDex user, that user holds them. Contact them directly, or email us and we will help.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, SnapDex will ask you to read and accept the new version in the app.
